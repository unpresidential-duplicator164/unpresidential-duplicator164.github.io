✨ Key Features

sparkle is built around two things you care about: your images and your prompts. Here is what you can do:

  • Store images privately: Keep all your reference images, generated art, and visual notes in one place. Files stay on your computer.
  • Save AI prompts: Write, edit, and organize prompts for AI art tools. No more losing good ideas in random text files.
  • Add metadata: Tag images and prompts with keywords, descriptions, and notes so you can find them later.
  • Search instantly: Find any image or prompt by name, tag, or text. Works completely offline.
  • Organize into collections: Group related images and prompts together for projects or themes.
  • Offline-first: sparkle works without an internet connection. Your data never leaves your device.
  • Clean, modern interface: Built with Svelte and Tailwind CSS for a smooth, responsive experience.

🖥️ System Requirements

sparkle is lightweight and runs on most modern Windows computers. Here is what we recommend:

  • Operating system: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
  • Memory: 4 GB RAM or more
  • Storage: 500 MB free disk space for the application and your library
  • Display: 1280 x 720 resolution or higher
  • Browser: Any modern browser (Edge, Chrome, Firefox) for downloading the file

You do not need a powerful graphics card or a high-end processor. sparkle is designed to be efficient and fast on everyday computers.

📖 How to Use sparkle

Once sparkle is open, you will see a simple dashboard with two main areas: Images and Prompts. Here is a quick walkthrough:

  1. Add your first image: Click the "Add Image" button, choose a file from your computer, and give it a name. You can add tags and notes to describe it.
  2. Create a prompt: Go to the Prompts section and click "New Prompt." Type your prompt text, add a title, and save it. You can also link prompts to images.
  3. Organize with collections: Create a collection (like "Fantasy Art" or "Portrait Prompts") and drag items into it. Collections help you keep related work together.
  4. Search and filter: Use the search bar at the top to find anything by name, tag, or text. You can also filter by collection or date added.
  5. Edit and update: Click any image or prompt to open it, then change its details, tags, or notes. Everything is saved automatically on your computer.

There is no save button to worry about. sparkle saves your changes as you go, so you can close the app and come back later without losing anything.

🔒 Privacy and Security

Your privacy is the whole point of sparkle. Unlike cloud-based libraries, sparkle does not send your images, prompts, or metadata to any server. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no tracking.

All data is stored locally on your Windows computer. You can back up your library by copying the sparkle data folder to an external drive. Because everything is offline, you can use sparkle on a plane, in a cafe without Wi-Fi, or anywhere else.
